10 / FINE PRINT, PLAIN WORDS
Is the app free to download?
Yes. You pay only when you confirm a shoot plan. Browsing, support and chat on an active booking are free.
How fast will crew respond?
Paid requests notify nearby available crew instantly. Most get a first response within minutes; the studio also monitors stuck requests.
Can I cancel or reschedule?
Yes, from Booking Details. Over 48 hrs: full refund. 24–48 hrs: half. Under 24: the crew turned down other work, so no refund. Full terms §5.
How does crew verification work?
ID, portfolio, experience, gear, location — five checks. Only VERIFIED crew receive paid requests, and suspensions show publicly.
Which phones are supported?
Android 7.0 and above. Push notifications need Google Play services.
How do I delete my account?
In-app: Profile → Delete Account. Or email famebros.studio@gmail.com with “Delete my FameGo account”. Details →
