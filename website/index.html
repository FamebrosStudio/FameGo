04SCENE · FAQ

Asked often.

Is the app free to download?
Yes. You pay only when you confirm a shoot plan. Browsing, support and chat on an active booking are free.
How fast will crew respond?
Paid requests notify nearby available crew instantly. Most get a first response within minutes; the studio also monitors stuck requests.
Can I cancel or reschedule?
Yes, from Booking Details. Refund windows depend on proximity to shoot time — see Terms §5.
How does crew verification work?
Crew submit city, role, experience, gear and portfolio. The studio reviews and marks them Verified before they receive paid requests.
Which phones are supported?
Android 7.0 (API 24) and above. Push notifications need Google Play services.
How do I delete my account?
In-app: Profile → Delete Account. Or email famebros.studio@gmail.com with “Delete my FameGo account”. Details in Privacy.
